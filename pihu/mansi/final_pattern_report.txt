**5. Statistical Test Results**

A chi-squared test reveals a significant association between 'Segment' and 'Category' (p < 0.01).

**6. Segment-Level Observations**

* Consumers (52.1% of sales) prefer Office Supplies, with a focus on Binders and Paper.
* Corporates (30.1% of sales) opt for Technology products, with a emphasis on Furnishings.
* Home Office customers (17.8% of sales) show a balanced preference for Office Supplies and Furniture.

**7. Time Trends**

Analysis of 'Order Date' and 'Ship Date' reveals a consistent sales pattern throughout the year, with no significant seasonal fluctuations.

**8. Business Risks & Data Limitations**

* The dataset's US-centric nature may limit the applicability of findings to other regions.
* The lack of ID columns hinders tracking individual customer behavior and loyalty.
* The dataset may not capture external factors influencing sales, such as marketing campaigns or competitor activity.

3. **Regional Expansion**: Explore opportunities to expand into other regions, leveraging the insights gained from the US market.
4. **Customer Loyalty Program**: Implement a customer loyalty program to track individual customer behavior and encourage repeat business.
